- [SHOULD FIX] `docs/research/period-rulings-generated-scripts.md` section 6.5, `data/custom-cards/c600000006.lua`, `data/errata/green-baboon-defender-of-the-forest.json` — Green Baboon part (a) was corrected although two period sources conflict, which the brief says to leave alone. Brief part C: "any conflict between period sources is a thin-evidence call. Change no data for it." I opened both. Konami's errata list compiled 2010-01-05 (capture 2010-03-31): "You cannot activate the effect of this card during the Damage Step." The Konami-hosted card FAQ (capture 2008-12-15, F-H page): "This effect can be activated during the Damage Step, at the same time as effects like "Giant Rat's"." The research document admits the conflict and resolves it "by order and authorship". That may be right, but the brief did not give the Builder that call. The consequence is that the Edison and Tengu Green Baboon now use no Damage Step, and this was not put to the owner. The builder's Open questions do not list it either (they mention it only as a "further finding"). Suggest Brain either accept it explicitly as an owner decision or put it on the thin-evidence list. In either case `rulings_check` for `c600000006` does not record the FAQ's "can be activated during the Damage Step" entry, since the gate only covers the face-up difference.
- [SHOULD FIX] `retroformats/validate.py` `_check_rulings` (around line 2924) — the gate is weaker than the brief's wording. The brief says the validator "rejects a record that cites a contradicting ruling and does not name an owner decision accepting it". The implementation rejects that only when the record itself marks the ruling `in_force: shown`. A `contradicts` finding marked `not-shown` passes with a warning and needs no `owner_decision`. Failure path: an author who wants a contradicted script to ship writes `not-shown` and the validator stays at 0 errors. The Builder flagged this choice openly and it is needed for Goddess of Whim and Green Baboon to remain, so it is a design call for Brain, not an error. No existing rule is loosened, but the brief's stated behaviour is only partly met. The gate also cannot check that a quoted passage is real; that is inherent and not a defect.
- [NOTE] Soul Rope, Rise of the Snake Deity (`data/errata/soul-rope.json`, `rise-of-the-snake-deity.json`) — the Damage Step corrections rest on the general Konami rulebook rule (Versions 7.1 and 7.2 and the 2011 capture, which I read: "During the Damage Step, you can only activate Counter Trap Cards, or cards with effects that directly change a monster's ATK or DEF."). For Snake Deity a UDE Netrep answer agrees, which I read at the archived thread: "cannot be activated in the Damage Step". For Soul Rope nothing card-specific was found; the same Netrep says a "small handful" of Traps had special privileges, so absence of an exemption is inferred from a general rule, and the OCG FAQ line is Yugipedia-only and OCG. It is consistent with the brief's "primary ruling in force contradicts" test, but it is the thinnest of the four corrections. In practice the list result is the same either way because the modern Soul Rope cannot answer battle destruction.
