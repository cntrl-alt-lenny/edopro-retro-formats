## Not verified

- Malefic's erratum date. The record dates the corrected text from the Silver Dragon Value Box reprint (NA 2013-09-13), and Yugipedia's DPKB set page says the English set was reprinted with Problem-Solving Card Text in that box, Duelist Pack: Yugi & Kaiba Special Edition and the collector boxes. But Yugipedia's errata table for the card also lists the corrected wording under "DPKB-EN023, Unlimited Edition" together with the Value Box, and I did not establish when the English Unlimited Edition appeared. If it carried the corrected text before 2011-09-17, Tengu's state would differ from Edison's; I found no evidence that it did.
- A real EDOPro client loading `dist/databases/retro-formats.cdb` and `dist/scripts/`; only the headless engine ran.

## Verdict

The engineering is sound and I have no doubt about the data side. The six records change only coverage and one metadata entry, each period text matches Yugipedia's first printing, the validator puts state `[]` at both Edison and Tengu for every card, both lists change by exactly the six expected lines, GOAT is untouched, `validate` and `build --check` match the brief, the pins and materializer test are exact, and CI plus my own engine mutation runs show every card's test failing on the modern behaviour under both formats. The problem is originality, which the brief made the second thing to check. Goddess of Whim (0.72) and Soul Rope (0.71) score above the removed Night Assailant script (0.69) and follow Ignis's structure function for function, so I do not think they should merge as original work. Green Baboon (0.61), Rise of the Snake Deity (0.58) and Strike Ninja (0.51) share Ignis's skeleton and a couple of specific API choices that no record explains, which is a smaller version of the same concern. Malefic Blue-Eyes is clean and could ship on its own. I am confident in the measurements, moderately confident that the two blocked scripts are adaptations (short scripts, standard idioms, no proof of how they were written), and it is not mine to decide the licence question. The cheapest way forward is for Brain and the owner to hold Goddess and Soul Rope back, decide on the middle three, and merge Malefic (plus the path) in a corrected round.
